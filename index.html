When an urge shows up

You can use this plan without judging yourself.

First: create a pause

Urges can feel urgent, but you can delay acting on them while you decide what you want to do next. You do not need to argue with the urge or make it disappear instantly.

60 seconds

Put both feet on the floor. Breathe gently and notice five things around you.

1
Name what is happening
“I’m noticing an urge.” Identify the feeling without treating it as an instruction.
2
Change your immediate environment
Stand up, leave the private or triggering space, put your device down, or move to a shared area if safe.
3
Make the next ten minutes easier
Take a short walk, drink water, shower, stretch, do a simple task, or contact a trusted person.
4
Reduce access to your trigger
Close the content, use available content restrictions, and avoid browsing alone in the situation that usually makes things harder.
5
Review without shame
Later, consider what preceded the urge and what support or change might help next time.
